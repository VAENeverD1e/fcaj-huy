# Summary Report: "AWS Study Tour & Cloud AI Journey"

### Event Objectives

- Provide Swinburne Vietnam students with an experiential visit to the AWS Vietnam office.
- Deliver a comprehensive overview of the Cloud Computing job market, hiring trends, and career roadmaps for early talents.
- Highlight the key differences between academic coursework and real-world enterprise engineering (Data Architecture, Infrastructure).
- Cultivate professional mindsets, soft skills (Communication, Small Talk), and effective AI leverage in the modern tech era.

### Speakers

- **Nguyễn Gia Hưng** - Head of Solution Architect @ AWS Vietnam (Founder @ AWS First Cloud AI Journey)
- **Bành Cẩm Vĩnh** - Data Engineer @ Renova Cloud (AWS Data Community Builder)
- **Chị Như Trần** - Account Manager @ AWS Vietnam (Startup Segment)
- **Khang Nguyễn** - Solution Architect @ Cloud Kinetics (Swinburne Alumnus)
- **Nguyễn Trần Minh Duy** - Industry Liaison Officer @ Swinburne Vietnam

### Key Highlights

#### Session 1: Job Market & Career Trends in Cloud Computing (Nguyễn Gia Hưng)

- **Hiring Challenges**: Escalating market requirements for early talents/interns (demanding Kubernetes proficiency, AWS certifications, English fluency, and hands-on side projects).
- **Cloud-First Shift**: Rapid growth of the Vietnamese Cloud market (AWS revenue grew 20x over 6 years while legacy hardware spending declined 10x).
- **Power of Community & Referrals**: Over 90% of Cloud job opportunities stem from internal networks and technical communities rather than public job boards.
- **Formula for Success**: $Value = Capability \times Visibility \times Consistency$ combined with 6 Mental Models (Teamwork, Hands-on, Building, Troubleshooting, Resilience, Invest in Yourself).

#### Session 2: From Campus to Enterprise - Real-world Data Architecture (Bành Cẩm Vĩnh - Renova Cloud)

- **Academic vs. Enterprise Reality**:
  - *Academic*: Clean datasets, well-defined requirements, flexible deadlines, minimal risk.
  - *Enterprise*: Fragmented/missing/unstructured data, shifting requirements, tight deadlines, high business impact.
- **DNA of a Data Platform**: Unified architectural framework across platforms (Ingestion, Processing, Storage, Governance, Catalog, Analytics).
- **Pragmatic AI Usage**: Leveraging AI (Kiro, Claude) for code reviews and automation while mastering fundamental engineering principles.
- **Field Takeaways**: Aligning closely with business requirements ("Start with the problem").

#### Session 3: Self-Positioning & Overcoming Workplace Fears (Chị Như Trần - AWS Vietnam)

- **Overcoming Psychological Barriers**: Identifying and dismantling the fear of failure, fear of judgment, fear of public speaking, and pressure of expectations.
- **Communication & Small Talk**: Bridging gaps with leaders and colleagues through proactive, genuine, and courageous interactions.
- **Opportunity Mindset (Blue Ocean)**: Persistence in pursuing goals (sharing the story of applying 11 times to AWS), seeking unconventional opportunities, and turning work into a journey of self-discovery.

#### Session 4: Skills & Mindset for Freshers in the AI Era (Khang Nguyễn - Cloud Kinetics)

- **Core AI Principle**: "You can outsource your thinking, but you cannot outsource your understanding" — Use AI to accelerate productivity, but thoroughly understand the underlying technology.
- **5 Candidate Evaluation Criteria**: Attitude (Top 1 priority), Aptitude, Experience, Exposure, and Talent.
- **Sustainable Growth Mindset**: Constantly asking "Why", embracing mistakes as learning opportunities, expanding beyond academic curricula, and maintaining long-term consistency.

### Key Takeaways

#### Career & Mindset

- Build long-term consistency and commit to lifelong learning ("Invest in yourself").
- Enhance community visibility to unlock unadvertised job opportunities through referral networks.

#### Architecture & Technical Execution

- Understand key trade-offs between academic projects and production-grade systems (Data Quality, Scalability, High Availability, Security).
- Master fundamental architectural frameworks (Data Platform DNA) rather than overly relying on specific tools.

#### Soft Skills & AI Adoption

- Communication is a core technical skill required to bridge gaps between business and technology.
- Treat AI as a productivity amplifier while retaining critical thinking and ownership.

### Applying to Work

- **Upgrade Side Projects**: Incorporate production standards (Data Quality, Exception Handling, Security, Documentation) into coursework and personal projects.
- **Engage in Technical Communities**: Actively participate in meetups and technical events (AWS FCJ, Study Groups) to expand networks and knowledge.
- **Practice Critical Thinking & Questioning**: Apply structured reasoning when interacting with AI and collaborating on team projects.

### Event Experience

Attending the **AWS Study Tour** provided an eye-opening and inspiring experience for students:

- **Real-World Tech Environment**: Visiting the AWS Vietnam office firsthand, engaging directly with leading industry experts.
- **Candid & Practical Insights**: Speakers provided transparent reflections on market conditions, empowering students to build realistic career plans.
- **Interactive & Engaging Atmosphere**: Lively Q&A sessions, direct discussions, and small talk demos made the event welcoming and impactful.
